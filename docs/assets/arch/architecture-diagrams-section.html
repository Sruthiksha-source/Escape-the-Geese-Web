Design evolution

Our architecture went through three versions: CRC cards (October), first class and sequence diagrams (30 October), and the final design (12 November). The earlier versions are kept below, unchanged in content, as evidence of how the design developed. Section 3.2 of Arch1.pdf explains each change and the requirement behind it.

CRC cards, version 1 (October 2026)

Superseded. Game held eight responsibilities, Player asked "Health bar ?", and Score ended the game at 100% of geese dispersed. Version 2 split Game into Main and four screens, replaced health with an ExhaustionMeter, and moved the win to "every wave cleared".

Class diagrams, version 1 (30 October 2026)

Superseded on 12 November. Player and Goose had health and Goose lost health from items; one Game class ran timer, waves, events and the win check; classes had no relationships drawn. Replaced by Figure 1 above.

Sequence diagrams, version 1 (30 October 2026)

Superseded on 12 November. Six overlapping scenarios built around the old Game class, including damage to the player and an "if item harms geese" branch. Merged into Figures 2 and 3 above, with Figure 4 added for goose behaviour.
